Our story

A cafe for people who care about the little things.

We created Cafe Mania as a welcoming place in the city: somewhere to meet friends, work by a window, or take a great coffee to go.

  • We choose beans from local roasters.
  • We bake fresh pastries in small batches every morning.
  • We use seasonal ingredients for new flavors.
